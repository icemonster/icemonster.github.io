Overview

In my doctoral degree at Carnegie Mellon University and Instituto Superior Tecnico, I specialized in fuzzing, a technique for discovering software vulnerabilities by generating malformed or unexpected inputs and feeding them to programs. My goal is to increase the security of software by developing automated tools for vulnerability detection.
